# Enquiry follow-up installation pilot

Draft commercial offer. Proposed price, not validated customer demand or a signed contract.

## Customer outcome

Your real commercial enquiries reach a named person, have a recorded next step, and appear in an internal exception report when that step is missing or overdue. The pilot is suitable for residential agencies, multifamily broker teams and property management businesses receiving owner enquiries.

## Proposed price

- Setup: **$750** for one bounded installation.
- Ongoing care: **$149/month**, separately agreed after handover.
- Existing software, n8n hosting, messaging/API usage and taxes are separate. No third-party expenditure or subscription is automatically authorised by this proposal.

This price is our initial test offer. It is not an industry benchmark, validated willingness to pay or a promise of profitability.

## Included scope

One authorised intake source (a website form or an accessible CRM/mailbox feed), one destination working system, one named-owner routing policy, approved reply templates, a recorded next action and one internal exception report. Include field mapping, idempotency/deduplication, closed/opted-out suppression, execution-error reporting, acceptance testing and a written handover.

The ongoing care proposal covers monitoring that configured workflow, troubleshooting connector failures and up to one hour of minor configuration changes per month. New sources, CRM migrations and redesigns require a separate quote. The final delivery schedule depends on access to the customer's accounts and the connector capabilities actually available.

Before accepting a pilot, verify access, API limits, platform licences and the availability of response/activity history. Narrow or decline the installation if those prerequisites cannot be met. Do not accept a payment for unavailable functionality.

## Three concrete journeys

- Residential: a viewing or seller enquiry receives an accountable owner and a recorded call/appointment next step.
- Multifamily: a buyer or seller enquiry reaches the responsible broker and gets a documented follow-up.
- Property management: an owner's management-service enquiry reaches the person responsible for acquisition and gets a recorded consultation next step.

These are the proposed installed journeys. The repository's local browser audit currently reviews supplied records and working-copy edits; it does not execute them in external systems.

## Acceptance and metrics

Use the customer's own records to establish an initial baseline. Check owner assignment, recorded first responses, completed next actions, appointment outcomes, connector failures and actual staff time. Use an agreed response target and working-hours policy. Missing recorded activity is verified against the source before it is treated as an unanswered enquiry.

Verify the workflow with consented test inputs before authorising real delivery. Record the operational result separately from sales results. Additional appointments, mandates, commissions and revenue depend on factors outside this installation and are not guaranteed.

No tenant screening, protected-trait ranking, MLS scraping, legal decisions, payments, cold-message blasting or purchased contact lists are included.
